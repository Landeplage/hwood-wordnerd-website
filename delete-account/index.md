# Delete your Wordnerd 3D account

Wordnerd 3D is made by Hållingwood A/S. You can delete your account, and the personal data tied to
it, at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Wordnerd 3D and tap the gear icon to open **Options**.
2. Tap **Account**, then **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. If your account has a password, you'll be asked for it first.

The account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email us at <{{ site.privacy_email }}> with the subject
"Delete my account". Send it from the email address on your account, or include your username if
you play as a guest. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, and we delete it within
30 days of that.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your sign-in details (email address, password, Sign in with Apple), avatar,
  friends and blocked players, notifications and push notification tokens. You're signed out on
  every device.
- **Anonymized:** your username is replaced with a placeholder that can't be traced back to you.
  Games you've played, and the chat messages in them, stay in your opponents' game histories under
  that placeholder.
- **Kept:** purchase receipts for Wordnerd Premium, for 5 years, as Norwegian bookkeeping law
  requires. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted.

Deleting your account doesn't refund a purchase. Premium belongs to the account, so it ends with
it.
