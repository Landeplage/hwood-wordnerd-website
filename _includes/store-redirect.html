{%- comment -%} Sends iOS and Android visitors straight to their app store, so a QR-code scan lands them one tap from the install prompt instead of on a page they have to read first. Opt in per page with `store_redirect: true` in front matter. Deliberately runs *after* the page has rendered and painted, and is included at the end of rather than in . Handing off from opens the store over a document that never received any content, so dismissing the store sheet ("Not now") drops the visitor on a blank page. Letting the fallback card paint first means that card is what's waiting behind the sheet. The sessionStorage guard makes the auto-launch a once-per-tab event. Without it, coming back from a dismissed store sheet re-triggers the handoff and the visitor is yanked straight back into the store, unable to reach the page. Once it has fired, the App Store badge on the card is the way back in. Because this no longer pre-empts lang-detect.html in , a visitor whose browser prefers another language is routed to their translated copy first (pre-paint, so it's invisible) and the fallback behind the store sheet is localized — which is what we want. site.play_store_url_android is blank until Android ships, so Android visitors currently fall through to the fallback card with everyone else. {%- endcomment -%}
