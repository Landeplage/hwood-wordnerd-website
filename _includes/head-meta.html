{%- comment -%} Title, description, social cards and language alternates for the current page. Titles/descriptions resolve in this order: 1. page front matter — long-form pages (press, privacy, terms) 2. t[tkey] — pages whose copy lives in _data/i18n 3. t_en[tkey] — fallback for an untranslated key 4. the site title/description `ref` and `tkey` come from i18n.html.
