Wordnerd 3D

The word game where you build words in three dimensions. Stack and spell with your friends!

How it plays

A familiar game with a new dimension

Any direction

Play words horizontally, vertically, and into the depth of the board.

Rotate the board

Find clever spots to play your words for maximum points.

Take your time

Turn-based matches you can play in a minute, or stretch over days.

About

Made in Norway

Wordnerd 3D is built by Hållingwood, a small studio that wanted to find out what would happen if you took the most beloved word game in the world and added a third dimension. Turns out: a lot of fun!
